30+ Years of Trusted, High-Quality Dairy Farming

Yenai Dairy Farm is one of the oldest farms in Kombolcha, Amhara Region, Ethiopia. For two generations, our family has been farming and providing dairy products such as fresh milk, cheese, and yoghurt to the local community. We also offer home delivery and are working on serving our products at our café. Today, we are looking to grow the farm and explore new ventures while continuing the family tradition we have built over the years.
